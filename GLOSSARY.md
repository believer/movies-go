# Movies

A personal movie diary: which movies were watched, when, with whom, and what they were rated.

## Language

**Movie intake**:
Bringing a movie into the diary from TMDB, or refreshing it, with its cast, crew, genres, languages, countries, production companies and awards.
_Avoid_: add movie, import, api

**Watch**:
A single viewing of a movie by a user on a date, possibly with friends.
_Avoid_: seen, view

**Rating**:
A user's score for a movie. A user can rate a movie again, and the latest rating is the one that counts.

**Review**:
A user's written opinion of a movie, public or private. One per user and movie.

**Series**:
An ordered group of movies, such as a franchise. A movie's number in a series belongs to the movie, not to any user.

**Now playing**:
A watch in progress: a movie a user has started but not finished, with a playback position.
_Avoid_: now watching, playback

**Watchlist**:
Movies a user intends to watch. A watch removes the movie from the watchlist.
